# 06 - Agriculture

The growth of crops requires suitable seasonal and humidity conditions, so their growth rate will vary with changes in solar terms and humidity.

Generally speaking, since most crops are suited to moderate humidity and most regions do not have extreme humidity, it is usually enough to simply plant crops that are adapted to the season at the right time.

This is what is meant by following the rhythm of nature.

But if we urgently need to grow crops that are unsuitable for the current environment, we will need to build a greenhouse.

## Build a Greenhouse

There are no strict requirements for building a greenhouse — it only needs to be a relatively enclosed space. It is generally recommended to use solid, light-permeable materials for the walls and ceiling, such as glass.

To ensure crops can grow in all seasons, we need to use a seasonal core corresponding to the crop's preferred season. For example, for wheat, you can use either the spring or summer core.

The core can be crafted from the corresponding essence, and can also be inserted with a right-click or removed with a sneak-right-click.

By default, seasonal essences are obtained by completing advancements or seasonal quests, but it can also be gained through seasonal rituals using a Seasonal Prayer Scroll; if in mod packs, follow the quest instructions.

Occasionally, humidity conditions may not be suitable for crop growth. When this happens, place humidity adjustment blocks nearby. You can search for them in JEI.

Using a growth detector can help check whether crops are suitable for this greenhouse.

## Seasonal Animals

*This is an optional feature.*

Animals may also have breeding season restrictions — note that the actual season depends on the region, so refer to the [Regional Differences](./region.md) entry. One notable change is that you can no longer milk cows at any time; it must be during their breeding season.

Another change concerns bees: bees only collect nectar in spring and do not leave their hives in winter.

As for fishing, things are much simpler — please give the fish some rest. If it’s not summer or the weather isn’t good, your fishing yields will be reduced.
