# 04 - Winter

Winter marks the end of the year — the warm days have passed, and now it is the season of snow.

However, only truly cold places can collect snow. In slightly warmer areas, snow is merely decorative, and rivers will not freeze. Although snow can be collected with a cauldron, the efficiency is ultimately too low.

Still, my friend, let us sit by the fireplace together, watching the snowflakes dance across a land blanketed in white.

The first solar term of winter is **Beginning of Winter**. At this time, it is not very cold and may not even snow — just be careful of the chilly winter rain. Next come **Light Snow** and **Heavy Snow**, when most biomes will begin to see snowfall in succession.

After that is **Winter Solstice**, the longest night of the year, though not the coldest. We must still pass through **Lesser Cold** and **Greater Cold**, the coldest time of the year. Even in some hot regions, if you are lucky enough, you may see snow.
