# 05 - Regional Differences

You may have noticed that there are also some climatic differences between regions here. The most significant difference is in rainfall frequency (If the setting is enabled, then even different biomes will have different weather), which greatly affects humidity conditions — for example, the wet and dry seasons of savannas, or the evenly distributed rainfall throughout the year in oceanic regions.

In addition, there are actual seasonal differences, which are easy to tell apart.

In hot regions, summers are longer; in cold regions, winters are longer. These factors can affect crop growth and animal reproduction (if applicable). 

As for snowfall, it generally depends on the annual average temperature of the region — sometimes in autumn, but more often only in winter.

Different regions’ landscapes also vary accordingly: some remain unchanged all year round, while others have distinct seasons. You are free to find a place you like.
