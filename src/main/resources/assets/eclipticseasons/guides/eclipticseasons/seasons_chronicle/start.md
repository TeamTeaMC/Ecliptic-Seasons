# 00 - Prologue

In the beginning, heaven and earth opened; the sun traveled back and forth, the four seasons alternated, cold and heat succeeded each other. Thus arose the great cycle, divided into twenty-four parts.

The way of heaven is such that even at its peak, it does not last long — a slight delay, and it begins to wane.

Therefore, the Summer Solstice is not the hottest time, and the Winter Solstice is not the coldest day.
