# 02 - Summer

Summer is the time when heat energy is at its peak, with scorching sunlight baking the land. Around midday, it is best to avoid activity under the sun to prevent heatstroke. ~~*Perhaps you could use a fire resistance potion, carry something to cool down, or wear a sun hat?*~~

In addition, humidity conditions also change in summer: biomes that are not very humid may become dry, while those that are already humid may become even more damp.

After **Beginning of Summer** comes **Lesser Fullness**, when grains begin to ripen.

During **Grain in Ear**, rainfall eases slightly, and crop growth reaches its peak. By **Summer Solstice**, the year’s longest day arrives, after which daylight hours gradually shorten.

Next are **Lesser Heat** and **Greater Heat**, the hottest time of the year. Droughts may occur, and crop growth slows down as a result.

For biomes like sparse savannas, this is often the rainy season, bringing a burst of vitality.
