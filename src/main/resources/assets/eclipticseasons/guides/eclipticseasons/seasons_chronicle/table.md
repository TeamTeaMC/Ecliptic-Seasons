# 07 - Appendix

* **Summer:** Beginning of Summer, Lesser Fullness, Grain in Ear, Summer Solstice, Lesser Heat, Greater Heat

* **Autumn:** Beginning of Autumn, End of Heat, White Dew, Autumnal Equinox, Cold Dew, First Frost

* **Winter:** Beginning of Winter, Light Snow, Heavy Snow, Winter Solstice, Lesser Cold, Greater Cold
