# 01 - Spring

People often have a good impression of spring — it is the season of flowers and grass growing. From this time, heat energy begins to accumulate, and crops start to grow rapidly.

Spring is the boundary between winter and summer; it is neither too cold nor too hot, with just the right temperature. However, crops do not start growing quickly right at **Beginning of Spring**; usually after **Rain Water**, conditions become most suitable for crop growth, as rainfall increases and humidity becomes favorable.

After **Insects Awakening**, there may occasionally be thunderstorms, along with continuous light spring rain. By **Spring Equinox**, day and night are equal, and the cold has mostly dissipated. Of course, in hot regions the cold retreats earlier, while in cold regions it retreats later.

Next comes **Fresh Green**, with just the right sunshine — the perfect time for outings. After that is **Grain Rain**, bringing even more rainfall. Spring is ending and summer is approaching, and the grains sown in spring can be prepared for harvest.
